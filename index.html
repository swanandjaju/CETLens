Experimental and Unofficial Predictor

Important Disclaimer

This predictor is experimental and based only on marks data generated from response sheets voluntarily uploaded and checked through CETLens. The displayed percentile is not calculated using official CET authority data and may differ significantly from actual results. Prediction accuracy heavily depends on the number of users using the platform, the ratio of top scorers to low scorers, shift-wise participation imbalance, topper distribution, incomplete datasets, and overall sample representation across different score ranges. Since percentile calculation is relative in nature, even a small variation in the number of high scorers using the platform can substantially affect the predicted percentile, especially at higher score ranges. CETLens cannot guarantee the reliability, stability, or final accuracy of the displayed percentile, and users should not treat it as official, final, or admission-safe data.
